Executive Snapshot

Results did drop in October: paid results are pacing about 17% below September, part of which is Google tracking. October is the steepest step in a gradual decline since July, when paid results peaked; October is pacing about 31% below July. Total ad spend stayed close to September, so the drop does not come from a smaller budget overall. It comes from LASIK: its Facebook budget was reduced after the clinic reported few bookings from Facebook leads, and its cost per message rose as the main LASIK creatives fatigued without new LASIK content to replace them. After the LASIK ads were edited on 22 Oct, cost per message is already coming down. Presbyond and Cataract held up and got cheaper per message.

Why LASIK dropped

Facebook budget reduced after clinic feedback. The clinic reported few bookings from Facebook leads, so the LASIK Facebook budget was cut. Spend roughly halved (5,185 to 2,449 AED) and Facebook LASIK messages fell from 364 to 206, which accounts for most of the LASIK drop. On the ad side, Facebook is LASIK’s cheapest platform, at 11.89 AED per message against 19.35 on Instagram. Booking quality cannot be confirmed from ad data alone; with the clinic’s bookings by source, we can compare cost per booking on Facebook and Instagram and set the budget split on that basis.

Higher cost from creative fatigue. LASIK cost per message has risen every month since July: 11.56, 13.06, 15.70 and now 16.95 AED. The rise sits in the two LASIK ads that carry most of the budget: “Apr 12, 2026” went from 12.01 AED per message in July to 21.55 in October and “Jul 9, 2026” from 12.18 to 17.46. These ads have been running for months, and there has been no new LASIK content in the same style to replace them: short, direct videos with the doctor, or clear, straightforward videos with patients who have had LASIK. That kind of content has driven LASIK’s best results so far.

LASIK after the 22 Oct edit

When the client first reported lower leads, we edited the LASIK ads on 22 Oct and refreshed the audiences. In the week after, cost per message fell by 11% and messages per day rose at a similar daily spend. Instagram improved most. New LASIK creatives should take this further.

Presbyond & Cataract held up

Costs and landing page on Google

Real drop: cost per click has risen every month since July (4.94, 5.81, 6.75 and now 8.00 AED). Against September it rose in both the English-keyword and Arabic-keyword campaigns, so a similar budget bought 19% fewer clicks. The English-keyword Target CPA campaign also almost stopped serving (125 to 33 clicks).

Landing page change: when the landing page was updated, the call button was removed and a floating WhatsApp button was added, and the floating button is not tracked. Tracked WhatsApp conversions fell 57%, much more than clicks and in every campaign at once, which points to contacts going through the untracked floating button. Calls fell because the landing page no longer has a call button; the calls still counted come from visitors who move to other pages of the website and use the call button in the footer. Form conversions are stable. At September’s conversion rate, Google would show about 110 conversions for October instead of 62 on a full-month basis.

Notes & Insights

Overall Highlights

Key Wins

Presbyond more efficient

Cost per message fell from 15.24 to 13.78 AED, with messages almost flat on a lower budget.

New top ad

“en Jan 11, 2026” (Presbyond) was October’s top ad: 227 messages at 7.86 AED each.

LASIK improving after 22 Oct

After the LASIK ad edit and audience refresh, cost per message fell from 18.62 to 16.59 AED in one week.

Cataract cheaper

Cost per message fell from 22.38 to 18.36 AED.

Facebook held volume

Facebook messages stayed level with September at a lower cost per message.

Watch-outs

LASIK messages down

LASIK Meta messages fell 23% vs September on a full-month basis, continuing a decline since July.

LASIK Facebook budget

LASIK’s Facebook budget roughly halved after the clinic reported few bookings from Facebook, although Facebook is LASIK’s cheapest platform. Bookings by source are needed to confirm.

LASIK creative fatigue

The main LASIK ads are getting more expensive, with no new LASIK content to replace them.

Google costs & tracking

Cost per click is up 19% vs September and 62% vs July. After the landing page update the call button was removed and the new floating WhatsApp button is not tracked.

Next Steps

  • Share LASIK bookings by source so we can compare cost per booking on Facebook and Instagram and set the budget split.
  • Get new LASIK content in the style that performs best: short, direct doctor videos and clear patient videos.
  • Add tracking to the floating WhatsApp button on the landing page.
  • Add the call button back to the landing page, with call tracking.
  • Keep testing Snapchat targeting, creatives and lead form before adding budget.
